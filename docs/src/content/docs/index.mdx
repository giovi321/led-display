## What it does

The firmware drives a chain of WS2812B matrix panels as one logical display. It shows a single value or short message, posted to it over plain HTTP: a revenue figure, a temperature, whatever your automation sends. You describe how the panels physically hang on the wall (grid shape, chain order, rotation, wiring) in a graphical editor, and the firmware rebuilds its pixel mapping live. No recompile when the hardware changes.
